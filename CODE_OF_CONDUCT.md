# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We pledge to make participation in CopperMCP a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility, apologize when mistakes affect others, and learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the safety of hardware users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, insults, threats,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private design data, and sustained disruption of community work.

## Enforcement

Report abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through GitHub's
repository reporting tools. Reports are reviewed promptly, fairly, and confidentially. Maintainers
may remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant when necessary.
