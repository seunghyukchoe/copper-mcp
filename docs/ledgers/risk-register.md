# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Status / owner |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| R-001 | Malformed or oversized PCB input exhausts the parser. | Medium | High | Bounded reads, typed validation, fuzzing, no file execution. | Open / security |
| R-002 | Model output bypasses constraints or triggers excessive agency. | High | High | Allowlisted policy actions, deterministic geometry/DRC, separate apply authorization. | Open / architecture |
| R-003 | A candidate is applied to a board changed after routing began. | Medium | High | Content hashes, live revision recheck, atomic undoable commit. | Open / KiCad integration |
| R-004 | Dependency or GitHub Action compromise affects contributors/releases. | Medium | High | Minimal dependencies, Dependabot, audit, CodeQL, immutable action pins, scoped permissions, a non-publishing release dry run, and tag-only attestations/releases. | Mitigated / release |
| R-005 | Benchmark leakage or selected examples exaggerate quality. | High | Medium | Project-family splits, multiple seeds, publish failures, immutable benchmark ledger. | Open / research |
| R-006 | DRC-clean output is treated as electrically or manufacturably safe. | Medium | High | Explicit preview warnings, separate hardware validation records, missing-evidence checklists, physics/DFM gates, prototype measurements, and professional review before fabrication approval. | Open / documentation |
| R-007 | KiCad subprocess or report-schema drift yields unsafe or stale evidence. | Medium | High | Fixed arguments, timeout, bounded private report, fail-closed schema parsing, board revision recheck. | Mitigated / KiCad integration |
| R-008 | Random native identities or volatile exporter metadata obscure meaningful hardware-review diffs. | Medium | Medium | Semantic UUIDv5 identities, read-only temporary replay by default, exact snapshot hashes, and a separately authorized artifact refresh. | Mitigated / Audio Board Lab |
| R-009 | The narrow Board IR v0.1 KiCad subset is mistaken for general KiCad compatibility, causing unsupported geometry or rules to be ignored. | High | High | Fail closed with structured diagnostics, publish an explicit accepted/rejected matrix, keep constraints typed and separate, and expand coverage only with regression fixtures. | Open / Board IR |
| R-010 | Internal A* grid checks are mistaken for authoritative DRC or a general autorouter result, or Board IR obstacle counts amplify search work. | High | High | Candidate-only API; narrow support matrix; typed rejection of unmodeled geometry; explicit internal-metric naming; independent grid, expansion, obstacle-count, and obstacle-check ceilings with cancellation checkpoints; keep KiCad export, DRC, preview, MCP, and apply gates unimplemented until independently verified. | Open / routing |
| R-011 | Disposable candidate bytes are mistaken for applied copper or durable DRC evidence, or a route is serialized against mismatched source context. | Medium | High | Exact source/profile/snapshot binding, candidate hash and deterministic replay checks, bounded deterministic segment identities, full Board IR round trip, no writes or MCP/apply surface, and a separate future candidate-evidence contract. | Open / KiCad integration |
| R-012 | Disposable serialization bypasses caller output budgets, amplifies native-identity collision work, or misattributes the derivative to KiCad. | Medium | High | Revalidate converted content with caller limits; reject base-plus-patch total-object overflow; collect UUID/tstamp identities once; record CopperMCP writer/version provenance; retain bounded Board IR round-trip verification. | Mitigated / KiCad integration |
| R-013 | Rewriting a derivative changes the source revision and silently churns Board IR identities for modeled KiCad geometry without native IDs. | Medium | High | Require a native UUID/tstamp on every modeled source geometry object before serialization; reject revision-derived identities explicitly; preserve exact candidate/base-revision binding. | Mitigated / KiCad integration |
| R-014 | A DRC summary is misbound to a different candidate, source, or rule/library context, or inconsistent/mutable counts alter accepted evidence after validation. | Medium | High | Bind candidate, Board IR, raw source, patched board, patched context, and nested summary revisions; exact-replay serialize from one capture; recheck private and original live contexts; cross-check process status against report findings and type totals against aggregates; freeze copied summary counts; return detached serialization; keep candidate boards private and ephemeral. | Mitigated / KiCad integration |
| R-015 | A public route preview is mistaken for an applied, durable, or generally supported routing capability, or it discloses board geometry to a host that should not receive it. | High | High | Explicit `routed`/`not_routed`/`unsupported_board` status; typed non-echoing diagnostics and bounded conversion-code counts instead of raw adapter text; caller-supplied typed constraints only; workspace-confined bounded reads; wall-clock deadline over the existing integer ceilings; no file, job, export, or apply side effect; documented geometry-disclosure boundary so hosts can decline the tool; opt-in authoritative DRC that fails closed rather than returning unverified evidence. | Open / routing |
| R-016 | Structural Board IR inspection leaks proprietary design content, or per-service request validation drifts apart and opens a gap in one surface. | Medium | High | Counts, digests, units, and standard KiCad layer names only, with a regression test asserting no names, coordinates, UUIDs, or typed identities appear in the serialized document; one shared `request_boundary` module owning field, type, range, boolean, and character rules for every public service; fail-closed conversion reporting diagnostic codes rather than adapter text. | Mitigated / Board IR |
| R-017 | An obstacle model that under-approximates existing copper produces routes KiCad rejects, or a board mixing net classes is routed to the looser clearance. | Medium | High | Exact integer rectangles for axis-aligned pads and orthogonal segments; ceiling half-widths; bounding boxes for round shapes that over-approximate only; obstacle inflation by the stricter of the routed and obstacle net-class clearances; fail-closed rejection of arcs, zones, vias, off-axis rotations, diagonals, and partially routed nets; shared model with the Dijkstra optimality oracle; and a committed blocked-pad fixture whose detour is confirmed by real KiCad 10.0.5 DRC. | Mitigated / routing |
| R-018 | Removing the via rejection is mistaken for multilayer routing support, or a via's clearance is modeled from its drill rather than its copper. | Medium | Medium | Obstacle built from the outer diameter, never the drill; bounding box over-approximates only; via obstacles counted against the obstacle budget; a via on the routed net still fails closed as partial routing; ADR, roadmap, and routing baseline state that this removes one of three blockers, and the routing baseline carries the current per-net measurement rather than a headline number. | Mitigated / routing |
| R-019 | A stale KiCad fill cache or an under-approximated zone outline permits a short, while treating the full outline as physical copper is mistaken for fill-aware routing or real-board progress; high-vertex rings may also amplify work. | Medium | High | Never trust `filled_polygon`; conservatively occupy the complete simple outline; exact integer containment/intersection/rational-distance predicates; strictest routed-class, zone-net-class, and zone clearance; same-net zones rejected as partial routing; each zone counts against the object ceiling and every vertex/bounds/edge relation consumes the obstacle-check budget with cancellation; explicit 0/14 CopperTone re-measurement and separate future refill/freshness contract. | Mitigated / routing |
| R-020 | Public audio-project pages are mistaken for open datasets, causing restricted schematics or layouts to enter fixtures, or a low-level routing check is presented as circuit design, electrical safety, or fabrication evidence. | High | High | Record the two reviewed sites as reference-only and non-redistributable; never fetch them in the runner; accept executable fixtures only from one repository-confined bounded snapshot with exact artifact/licence hashes, SPDX licence evidence, derivation and safety fields, no third-party content, disjoint claims/non-claims, and claims derived from observed results; keep mains/high-voltage cases out of defaults and add schematic/ERC/simulation/hardware gates separately. | Mitigated / research |
| R-021 | Model-authored logical input injects KiCad syntax, exhausts parsing/rendering, creates ambiguous connectivity, collides deterministic identities, or is mistaken for an electrically or board-ready schematic. | High | High | Strict bounded schema and codec; unknown/duplicate/numeric rejection; typed references and complete pin ownership; canonical content digest; escaped strings; collision and deterministic replay tests; original embedded symbols; empty footprints and `on_board=no`; 1 MB pure-render cap; real KiCad parse/netlist verification; no MCP/CLI/file-writing surface; explicit ERC, value, parity, electrical, and fabrication non-claims. | Mitigated / architecture |
| R-022 | Public schematic delivery leaks proprietary topology into ordinary model context, treats a content digest as authorization, crosses clients over stateless HTTP, retains artifacts without bound, or overwrites a workspace file. | High | High | Redacted build record; independent opaque 256-bit capability; non-enumerable stdio-only process store with 15-minute lifetime, 16-entry/16 MiB aggregate and 1 MiB per-artifact ceilings, deterministic LRU, uniform unavailable response, digest recheck, and no persistence/logging; HTTP registration disabled; CLI requires an explicit workspace-confined create-new `.kicad_sch` target with symlink/traversal rejection and no overwrite mode; per-build ERC/electrical/parity/readiness non-claims. | Mitigated / delivery |
| R-023 | KiCad DRC inherits user-global configuration, plugins, library tables, temporary paths, or credential-bearing environment variables, making evidence host-dependent or exposing secrets to the child process. | Medium | High | Build the child environment from a minimal allowlist (`PATH=os.defpath`, `LANG/LC_ALL=C`) plus private per-run HOME, KiCad, XDG, runtime, and temporary roots; inherit no caller entries or KiCad overrides; reject state-tree symlinks and special files; enforce existing per-file, file-count, cumulative-byte, and scan-time ceilings. | Mitigated / KiCad CLI |
| R-024 | A future semantic/visual scene or placement agent is mistaken for authority and writes model-generated geometry directly into KiCad, or the observation leaks more design content than intended. | High | High | Versioned bounded Circuit Scene IR; capability-specific redaction; typed placement intent only; immutable revision-bound previews/candidates; deterministic snapping, connectivity, clearance, rule and provenance validation; explicit separately authorized apply; direct AI KiCad mutation remains prohibited. | Open / architecture |
| R-025 | A 15-minute artifact TTL is described as immediate deletion or secure memory erasure, while expired bytes, allocator pages, returned copies, crash dumps, or swap can persist beyond capability expiry. | Medium | High | Define TTL solely as an absolute access deadline; reads never renew it; lazily reclaim expired store entries on later reads/insertions and process exit; keep store count/byte caps; document that hosts own returned copies and that no secure-erasure guarantee exists. | Mitigated / delivery |
| R-026 | A workspace path is validated and then reopened after replacement, or a case-variant output suffix bypasses the intended create-only artifact boundary. | High | High | Open every input path component relative to one held workspace descriptor with no-follow semantics; use the same final descriptor for type/size validation, bytes, and before/after state checks; anchor output publication and verification to a held directory descriptor; accept only exact lowercase `.kicad_sch`; reject symlinks, parent replacement, existing files, and case variants. | Mitigated / filesystem |
| R-027 | A captured KiCad file table redirects DRC to environment-expanded, absolute, remote, or plugin-backed libraries outside the evidence snapshot, or the child resolves relative state from the live repository/workspace. | High | High | Parse and validate captured file-table URIs before execution; permit only dependencies confined to the private snapshot; reject environment, absolute, remote, and plugin schemes; run the minimal-environment child with private `TMPDIR` as cwd; retain private state/context budgets and post-run revision checks. | Mitigated / KiCad CLI |
| R-028 | MCP wrapper coercion or extra-field smuggling bypasses the intended Circuit Intent contract, or validation errors echo proprietary scalar/list content. | High | High | Close outer wrapper, nested content, and structured output schemas; reject scalar, list, and extra-field shapes at each boundary; return non-echo generic failures; retain redacted output regression checks. | Mitigated / MCP |
| R-029 | KiCad replaces a DRC report with a symlink/FIFO, blocks the reader, emits duplicate/non-finite/deep JSON, or writes unrecognized state elsewhere in the private design snapshot. | High | High | Open the report no-follow and nonblocking; require a regular file from descriptor metadata; enforce duplicate-key, non-finite, nesting-depth, and decoded-value limits; make the private snapshot tree read-only and validate the complete tree for every side effect before accepting evidence. | Mitigated / KiCad CLI |
| R-030 | A caller mutates an artifact object after insertion so the capability serves bytes that no longer match identity or aggregate byte accounting. | High | Medium | Snapshot exact immutable content, digest, and size into each store entry; use only stored size for quotas and eviction; verify stored digest against stored bytes on every read. | Mitigated / delivery |
| R-031 | A release tag is created from a commit without version-specific notes or an explicit record that its full gate authorized tagging. | Medium | High | Require a dated changelog section matching the version and an append-only `Ready` release-authorization row naming the exact clean-`make check` commit; treat authorization, tag creation, artifact build, and publication as distinct states. | Mitigated / release governance |
| R-032 | A silent coordinate-convention error in a source adapter places geometry plausibly but wrongly, so every downstream contract is exact about the wrong board. | Medium | High | The KiCad footprint quarter-turn map is pinned by a committed fixture whose expected pad positions are adjudicated by KiCad's own connectivity engine rather than by arithmetic in this repository; the adapter refuses footprint-local zones, footprint graphics on copper or Edge.Cuts, and non-orthogonal footprint transforms, so no other object class inherits the transform; a CopperTone test asserts no foreign track endpoint lies inside a foreign pad, which is the cross-check that exposed the original defect. | Mitigated / Board IR |
| R-033 | The committed CopperTone board's mounting-hole rule areas were generated as octagons inscribed at the required 2.85 mm keep-out radius, so their edges sit 0.2169 mm inside the requirement and copper may legally approach closer than the constraint intends. | Low | Medium | The generator now circumscribes the octagon so edges hold the full radius; a dedicated test records that the committed board still carries the older inscribed keepout; regeneration is deferred to its own slice because new coordinates invalidate every measurement recorded against the board's current revision digest, and that slice must update the recording test and re-measure coverage in the same change. | Open / hardware |
| R-034 | Board IR 0.2's rectangular front-courtyard support is mistaken for general footprint fidelity, or exposed courtyard geometry is mistaken for a completed placement-legality check. | High | High | Fail closed on back-side footprints, non-orthogonal transforms, mismatched layers, non-rectangular/open/filled/mixed courtyard primitives, and more than 64 rings; bind pose, ownership, side, lock, and canonical board-frame rings into the snapshot digest; charge relationships and vertices to request budgets; keep `courtyard_overlap` fixed at `not_modelled`; refuse locked moves; require source-oracle fixtures and a new contract review before expanding the subset or enabling placement apply. | Open / Board IR |
| R-035 | A Board IR layered candidate may be mistaken for KiCad-valid copper, or conservative lattice envelopes may under-model a physical edge/via/clearance rule. | High | High | Keep the adapter proposal-only and out of MCP, serializer, DRC, fill, and apply paths; verify snapshots and revisions; include via diameter in edge bounds; use separate via-radius envelopes and safe rotated-pad bounds; reject unsupported selected-net copper, arcs, constraints, and outlines; record B-018 with deterministic replay, no-path, stale, off-grid, source-immutability, and tamper evidence; require source-preserving round-trip serialization plus authoritative KiCad DRC before promotion. | Open / routing |
| R-036 | A non-orthogonal Board IR pad angle could be mistaken for an unsupported parent footprint pose, or a valid layered obstacle budget exhaustion could be collapsed into malformed input and trigger retry loops. | Medium | Medium | Keep parent poses restricted to four orthogonal turns, render pad rotations with exact decimal tokens, reparse the disposable placement candidate, and return a distinct `obstacle_budget_exceeded` diagnostic with a regression test. | Mitigated / placement and routing |
